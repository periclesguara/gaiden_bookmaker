## About This Edition

This Illustrated Modern English Edition of Conan the Conqueror: The Hour of the Dragon presents Robert E. Howard’s only full-length Conan novel in a refined, accessible, and carefully curated format for today’s readers.

Originally published in 1935, this epic tale stands as one of the greatest works of sword-and-sorcery fiction ever written. In this edition, the original public-domain text has been meticulously modernized in language while preserving its tone, intensity, and heroic grandeur.

The translation and adaptation have been crafted to enhance clarity and rhythm without altering the narrative structure or spirit of Howard’s prose. Archaic constructions have been gently refined to provide a smoother reading experience, while maintaining the dark majesty, political intrigue, and savage beauty that define Conan’s world.

This illustrated edition includes original artwork designed to amplify the atmosphere of Hyboria — its battlefields, shadowed kingdoms, and mythic landscapes — creating a fully immersive reading experience.

Editorial work has been carefully supervised to ensure consistency, linguistic precision, and aesthetic balance. The result is a premium edition that honors the legacy of Robert E. Howard while making his masterpiece accessible to modern audiences.

Whether you are discovering Conan for the first time or returning to one of fantasy’s foundational legends, this edition delivers a powerful, elegant, and unforgettable reading experience.
