## About This Edition

ABOUT THIS EDITION

Step into the very first Sherlock Holmes mystery—reborn in a premium illustrated edition crafted for modern readers.

Sherlock Holmes: A Study in Scarlet is the case that introduced the world’s most famous detective and his trusted companion, Dr. John Watson. From fog-drenched London streets to a haunting tale of vengeance that stretches far beyond England, this novel delivers the sharp deductions, suspense, and atmosphere that defined the detective genre.

This MantaQuest Illustrated Edition · Modern English was produced to offer a smoother, more immersive reading experience without losing the classic Victorian character of the original.

What you’ll find in this edition:
• Modern English refinement for improved clarity and flow  
• Premium illustrations designed to enhance mood and setting  
• Carefully formatted for Kindle and print readability  
• A consistent editorial standard for collectors and first-time readers alike
