As the fallen king stared with burning eyes that showed the bitter rage and humiliation consuming his heart, the squires removed Valannus’s chainmail shirt, helmet, and leg armor, then dressed him in Conan’s black plate armor, complete with the visored salade and the dark plumes nodding above the wyvern crest. Over everything they placed the silk surcoat embroidered with the royal lion in gold across the chest, and they fastened around him a wide belt with a gold buckle that held a jewel-hilted broadsword in a gold cloth scabbard. While they worked, trumpets blared outside, weapons clashed, and across the river came a deep roar as squadron after squadron moved into formation.  

Fully armed, Valannus dropped to one knee and bowed his plumes before the figure lying on the dais.  

“My lord king, may Mitra grant that I do not bring shame to the armor I wear today!”  

“Bring me Tarascus’s head and I’ll make you a baron!” In the torment of his anguish, Conan’s thin layer of civilization fell away. His eyes blazed; he ground his teeth in fury and bloodlust, as savage as any tribesman from the Cimmerian hills.
